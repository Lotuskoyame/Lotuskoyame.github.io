Nook

A calm notebook for characters, stories, worlds, ideas, and pictures, made to be easy on the eyes and easy to find your way around.

What Nook does

Nook is a notes app for Android phones and Windows computers. You can keep ideas, to-do lists, moodboards, characters with their pictures and relations, stories and comics, worlds, plots, reference boards, and sketchbooks. Everything is stored on your own device.

Google Drive sync

If you choose to, you can connect Nook to your own Google Drive so your notes stay the same on your phone and your computer. Nook only uses its own hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. You can turn sync off or switch accounts at any time in Nook's settings.

Privacy

Nook has no servers and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
