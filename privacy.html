Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

Nook keeps your notes on your own devices. If you turn on Google Drive sync, your notes are also kept in your own Google Drive. The people who make Nook never receive, see, store, or share your notes or any other data.

What stays on your device

Your notes, pictures, and settings are saved on the phone or computer where you use Nook. Backups you export are files that you control.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on. When you connect a Google account, Nook asks Google for two permissions:

  • See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Nook stores a copy of your notes and pictures in a hidden folder that only Nook can use. Nook cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • See your email address (userinfo.email). This is only used to show which account is connected in Nook's settings.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to anyone else. The sign-in key that keeps you connected is stored only on your device.

How Google data is used

Nook uses data from Google only to sync your own notes between your own devices. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone. Nook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Turn off sync in Nook's settings to stop syncing. To remove Nook's copy from your Drive and revoke its access, open your Google Account, go to Security, then Third-party apps with account access, choose Nook, and remove access. Nook's hidden folder can also be deleted from Google Drive settings under Manage apps. Notes on your devices stay until you delete them there.

Children

Nook does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
